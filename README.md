# Keenwiix Wellness

English | [Русский](README.ru.md)

**A landing page for a wellness brand that feels as calm as the product it sells, and invites visitors to begin their first ritual.**

Live demo: https://keenwiix.github.io/keenwiix-wellness/

## The problem

Wellness apps promise calm, then greet people with streaks, charts, badges and notifications. The first impression is more noise, and a person who came to slow down leaves before they have tried anything.

Wellness websites often copy the same pattern: crowded pages, big claims and no clear idea of what daily life with the product looks like.

## The solution

Keenwiix Wellness is a landing page that does what the brand promises. It is quiet, unhurried and clear. It shows in a few simple steps what the product helps you do, what a day with it looks like, and how to start, without overwhelming the visitor.

## What your visitors get

- **A calm first screen.** One clear line, "Slow down. Find your calm.", and one clear next step.
- **Four practices, easy to grasp.** Mood check-ins, meditation sessions from 3 to 30 minutes, small low-pressure habits, and sleep insights.
- **A brand with a point of view.** A short manifesto: "We don't need more data. We need more presence."
- **A look at the app.** A phone mockup with Today, Patterns, Rituals and Rest, and a "Morning calm" ritual.
- **Membership that is easy to choose.** Three plans, Free, Premium and Family, described in plain words.
- **Voices of real-feeling people.** A testimonials section that builds trust.
- **Answers to the doubts that stop people.** An FAQ about the free version, how mood tracking works, cancelling, privacy and working offline.
- **A gentle invitation.** The first ritual is free, and every call to action leads to a chat with your team in Telegram.

## What you get as the owner

- **Ready for your content.** Replace the name, plans, numbers and reviews with your own and the page keeps its structure and tone.
- **Ready for search and sharing.** Page title, description and Open Graph tags are already set.
- **Leads where you work.** Visitors reach you in Telegram, so there is no new inbox to check.
- **Fast and light.** A static site with no build step, which can be hosted for free, for example on GitHub Pages.
- **Room to grow.** A clear, small codebase that is simple to extend with new sections and pages.
