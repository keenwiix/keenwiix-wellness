FAQ

Questions?

Everything you might want to know.

Is the free version really free?

Yes — completely. No credit card needed, no hidden fees. You get mood tracking, 5 meditations, and basic habits forever.

How does mood tracking work?

Simply note how you're feeling each day. Over time, you'll see patterns and gain insights into what affects your wellbeing.

Can I cancel Premium anytime?

Yes — cancel anytime in one click. No questions, no waiting, no hidden process.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Your data is encrypted and never shared. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Does it work without internet?

Most features work offline — meditations, mood check-ins, and habits. Your data syncs when you're back online.
